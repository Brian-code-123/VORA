# VORA Bank demo FAQ (English)
Fictional bank used only to test retrieval on questions written by other people. Each line is one chunk: [ID] text. B01 and C01 state the same fact (English / Chinese).

[B01] Using your card abroad: tell us before you travel in the VORA Bank app under Cards > Travel notice, or call 0800 123 456. Your card works in 150 countries and the foreign transaction fee is 1.5%. A travel notice lasts up to 60 days.
[B02] Changing your address: update it in the app under Profile > Address and upload a utility bill dated within the last 3 months. The change takes 2 working days. New cards are always sent to your new address.
[B03] App errors: if the app shows an error, update to the latest version, restart your phone and log in again. If error code E-502 appears, wait 10 minutes. If it still fails, call app support on 0800 123 457, open daily from 8:00 to 22:00.
[B04] ATM limit: the daily ATM withdrawal limit is $500. You can raise it temporarily to $1,500 for 24 hours in the app under Cards > Limits. The limit goes back to $500 afterwards.
[B05] Checking your balance: see your balance on the app home screen, by texting BAL to 70123, or at any VORA ATM. Balances update in real time. Pending card payments are shown separately for up to 3 days.
[B06] Business loans: business loans start at $5,000 and go up to $250,000, with rates from 6.9% APR and terms of 1 to 7 years. Apply in the Business section of the app. You need 12 months of trading history. A decision takes 2 working days.
[B07] Card problems: if your card is declined, check that it is not frozen and that your balance covers the payment. A damaged card is replaced free of charge within 5 working days. Order the replacement in the app under Cards > Replace.
[B08] Cash deposits: deposit cash at any VORA ATM with a deposit slot or at a branch. Deposits up to $2,000 per day are available at once, larger amounts take 1 working day. There is no fee for cash deposits.
[B09] Direct debits: set up or cancel a direct debit in the app under Payments > Direct debits. A cancellation takes effect immediately, but you should also tell the company. The Direct Debit Guarantee refunds any payment taken in error.
[B10] Freezing your card: to freeze a lost or stolen card, tap Cards > Freeze in the app or call 0800 123 458 at any time. A freeze is instant and you can undo it in the app. A stolen card is cancelled for good and you order a replacement.
[B11] High value payments: payments over $10,000 need a confirmation code sent to your phone. If a payment is above $50,000 you must also call 0800 123 459. The daily limit for transfers is $100,000.
[B12] Joint accounts: both holders must apply, in the app under Accounts > Open joint account, or at a branch with ID for both people. Each holder gets a card and either can pay. Changes to the account need both holders to approve.
[B13] Latest transactions: see recent transactions in the app under Accounts > Activity, which shows the last 90 days. Statements for the last 7 years can be downloaded as PDF. Report any transaction you do not recognise within 60 days.
[B14] Paying a bill: pay a bill in the app under Payments > Pay a bill by entering the company name or reference number. A payment made before 16:00 on a working day arrives the same day. You can schedule recurring bills up to 12 months ahead.
