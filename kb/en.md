# VORA Box FAQ (English)
Each line is one chunk: [ID] text. Twin ids: E01 and Z01 state the same fact.

[E01] VORA-X200 hardware: the VORA-X200 (also called VORA Box X200) has a 4-core ARM CPU, 4 GB RAM and 32 GB internal storage. It is the flagship model and runs all speech and language models offline on the device.
[E02] VORA-M100 hardware: the VORA-M100 (VORA Box Mini) has a 2-core ARM CPU, 2 GB RAM and 16 GB storage. It supports English and Mandarin but has a smaller knowledge base limit of 256 MB.
[E03] Warranty: the VORA-X200 warranty period is 2 years. The VORA-M100 warranty period is 1 year. Power adapters and cables are covered for 6 months. Physical damage and water damage are not covered.
[E04] Wake word: the default wake word is "Hey Vora". You can change it in the VORA Home app under Settings > Wake word. Custom wake words need 3 training recordings.
[E05] LED colors: blue means the box is listening, green means it is speaking, red means the microphone is muted, and flashing white means it is resetting or updating.
[E06] Privacy: all audio is processed on the device. Audio and transcripts are never uploaded to the cloud. A hardware mute switch on top of the box physically disconnects the microphones.
[E07] Wi-Fi setup: open the VORA Home app, tap Add Device and follow the pairing steps. The X200 supports Wi-Fi 6 on 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz. The M100 supports Wi-Fi 5 on 2.4 GHz only. Wi-Fi is only needed for firmware updates and the app.
[E08] Factory reset: hold the reset button on the back for 10 seconds until the LED flashes white. This erases all settings and imported documents. Firmware is kept.
[E09] Firmware updates: VORA Box installs firmware over the air once a month. To check manually open VORA Home > Settings > About > Check for update. Never unplug the box while the LED is flashing white.
[E10] Languages: VORA Box understands and speaks Mandarin Chinese and English, and can answer in mixed Chinese and English sentences. Cantonese speech is not supported.
[E11] Custom knowledge base: you can import your own documents (Markdown, TXT, PDF) up to 1 GB on the X200 through the VORA Home app or a USB drive. The box uses them to answer questions offline. Product names and technical terms can be added to a glossary.
[E12] Price and returns: the VORA-X200 costs 199 USD and the VORA-M100 costs 99 USD. You can return either model within 30 days for a refund. Return shipping is paid by the buyer unless the unit is defective.
[E13] Support contact: email support@vora.example. Support hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 Hong Kong time. Please include the serial number printed under the box.
[E14] Operating conditions: use the box between 0 and 40 degrees Celsius and 10 to 90 percent humidity. Keep it away from heaters and direct sunlight.
[E15] Power: the X200 uses a 12 V 2 A USB-C adapter. The M100 uses a 5 V 2 A USB-C adapter. The X200 draws about 5 W when idle and 12 W while answering.
[E16] Microphones: the X200 has a 4-microphone array with a pickup range of 5 meters. The M100 has 2 microphones with a range of 3 meters. Both use noise suppression, but loud music can reduce accuracy.
[E17] Speaker: the X200 has a 10 W speaker and the M100 has a 5 W speaker. Volume is set by voice or in the VORA Home app.
[E18] Troubleshooting when the box does not respond: check that the LED is not red (muted), unplug the power for 10 seconds and plug it in again, move within the microphone range, and make sure you say the wake word "Hey Vora" clearly.
